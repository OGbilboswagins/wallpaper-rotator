Privacy Policy for Wallpaper Rotator

Last Updated: July 2026

Wallpaper Rotator is committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

Wallpaper Rotator does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Photos and Media Access

Wallpaper Rotator requests access to photos and images stored on your device solely so you can select wallpaper folders and rotate wallpapers.

Your photos remain on your device at all times.

Wallpaper Rotator does not upload, copy, or transmit your images to any server.

Purchases

Wallpaper Rotator offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade through Google Play Billing.

Purchase processing is handled entirely by Google Play. Wallpaper Rotator does not receive or store your payment information.

Analytics

Wallpaper Rotator does not currently collect analytics or usage statistics.

Advertising

Wallpaper Rotator does not display advertisements.

Third-Party Services

Wallpaper Rotator uses Google Play services for application distribution and optional in-app purchases.

These services are governed by Google's own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Wallpaper Rotator is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
